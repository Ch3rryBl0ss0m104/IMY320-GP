About StarBud

We build learning clubs kids already know how to use.

StarBud started with a simple idea: kids shouldn't have to relearn how to sign in every time they try something new. So we built a family of colourful, friendly clubs, one for each skill, that all share one easy account and one set of habits.

What we believe

Familiar beats fancy.

1

Easy feels good

When your login and profile work the same way in every club, you get to spend your energy on the fun part, making things, not figuring out buttons.

2

One club, one skill

Each club focuses on one thing and does it well, code, art, music, or numbers, instead of one big confusing site trying to do everything.

3

Friendly, always

Simple words, big buttons, and helpful messages when something goes wrong, never confusing, never scary.

4

One account, everywhere

One profile, one password, one place to see all your badges, no matter which club you're in today.
